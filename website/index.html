About KrisiBandhu

Your trusted partner in crop insurance

KrisiBandhu (meaning "Friend of Farmers") is a revolutionary digital platform designed to transform the crop insurance landscape in India. Built on cutting-edge AI and cloud technology, we're making insurance claims faster, more transparent, and fairer for farmers.

Our platform combines a mobile app for farmers, AI-powered image analysis, and a real-time monitoring dashboard for officials to create an end-to-end solution that benefits all stakeholders in the agricultural ecosystem.

🎯 Our Mission

To empower every Indian farmer with accessible, transparent, and efficient crop insurance solutions

💡 Our Vision

A future where crop insurance claims are processed instantly, fairly, and without bureaucratic hurdles
